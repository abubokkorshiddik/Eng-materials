How to study each lesson

Use the same routine with every tense to improve understanding and retention.

📘

1. Understand the rule

Learn when the tense is used, how sentences are formed, and how affirmative, negative, and question forms work.

✍️

2. Practise with examples

Study examples in context, compare similar tenses, identify common mistakes, and write your own sentences.

🎯

3. Apply it to IELTS

Use accurate tenses when describing trends in Writing Task 1, discussing experiences in Speaking, and interpreting reading passages.
