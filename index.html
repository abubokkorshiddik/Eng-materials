The ten topics

They are in a helpful order, from the building blocks to the finishing touches. Already comfortable with one? Jump straight to the one you need.

1. Sentence structure

Build complete, accurate sentences.

2. Subject–verb agreement

Avoid errors such as “people is” instead of “people are”.

3. Tenses

Choose the correct tense and keep time references consistent.

4. Articles and nouns

Use a, an, the, and plural forms correctly.

5. Complex sentences

Connect ideas clearly and accurately.

6. Relative clauses

Add information without creating multiple short sentences.

7. Conditionals

Discuss possibilities, consequences, and hypothetical situations.

8. Passive voice

Useful for academic writing and process descriptions.

9. Gerunds and infinitives

Avoid errors such as “I enjoy to read” instead of “I enjoy reading”.

10. Punctuation

Separate ideas correctly and avoid comma splices.

Inside the tenses topic

Tenses is the biggest topic, so it has its own set of lessons. Open the tenses page or follow this order:

  1. Present simple and continuous habits, facts, and what is happening now
  2. Past simple finished events and stories
  3. Past continuous what was going on in the background
  4. Future simple promises, decisions and predictions
  5. Future continuous what will be happening at a future time
  6. Present perfect the bridge between past and now
  7. Past perfect the earlier of two past events
  8. Future perfect finished by a future deadline
  9. Present perfect continuous how long, and the effects now
  10. Past perfect continuous duration before a past moment
  11. Future perfect continuous duration up to a future moment

How to get the most from each lesson

  • Read the plain-words paragraph first. It gives you the big idea before the rules.
  • Read the mistakes slowly. Most exam errors are on that list.
  • Do the practice before you look at the answers.
  • Keep a notebook of your own mistakes and read it every other day.
  • Take the quiz again after a day, three days and a week.
