Subject–verb agreement

Apply agreement rules to longer sentences, quantity expressions and IELTS-style grammar.

1. Ignore extra information

A phrase between the subject and verb does not usually change agreement. Find the head noun, then match the verb to it.

The list of required documents is on the website.
The documents on the list are required.
The director, together with two assistants, is attending.

2. Quantity expressions

ExpressionUsual agreementExample
the number ofSingularThe number of applicants is rising.
a number ofPluralA number of applicants are waiting.
each / every / either / neitherSingular in formal usageEach of the reports has a title.
both / several / many / fewPluralSeveral reports contain errors.
some / most / all / half ofDepends on the noun after “of”Most of the water is clean. Most of the samples are clean.

3. Subjects joined by or and nor

With either…or, neither…nor and similar pairs, the verb generally agrees with the nearer subject. For clearer writing, place the plural subject nearer the verb when possible.

Neither the assistants nor the manager is available.
Neither the manager nor the assistants are available.

4. Collective nouns

Words such as team, committee, family and staff may be singular when the group acts as one unit. British English also often uses plural agreement when members are considered individually. Keep pronouns consistent.

The team has announced its decision. (one unit)
The team are arguing among themselves. (members individually, common in British English)

5. Percentages, amounts and measurements

Use singular agreement when a measurement is treated as one total. With fractions and percentages, agreement normally follows the noun after of.

Three hours is enough time.
Thirty per cent of the land is forest.
Thirty per cent of the participants are students.

6. Relative clauses and “one of”

She is one of the researchers who work on this project. (who refers to researchers)
She is the only researcher who works on this project. (who refers to researcher)

7. Intermediate practice

1. The number of online applications ___ increased.

2. A number of applicants ___ withdrawn.

3. Most of the samples ___ contaminated.

4. He is one of the researchers who ___ here.

5. Three hours ___ enough for the exam.
