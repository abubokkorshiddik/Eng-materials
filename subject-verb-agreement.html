Subject–verb agreement

Learn how subjects and verbs agree in number, from the essential rules to complex academic sentences. Choose your level and progress step by step.

Choose your level

Beginner

Learn the basic rules for singular and plural subjects, including he/she/it, I/you/we/they, and common sentence patterns.

Start Beginner

Intermediate

Practise agreement with compound subjects, indefinite pronouns, collective nouns, intervening phrases, and quantities.

Start Intermediate

Advanced

Master difficult agreement patterns in academic writing, including inverted structures, relative clauses, and complex subjects.

Start Advanced

Recommended learning order

  1. Start with Beginner if you are still learning the basic rules.
  2. Move to Intermediate when you can identify the main subject confidently.
  3. Complete Advanced to practise complex sentences and IELTS Academic writing.

Learning goal

By completing all three levels, you should be better able to identify the true subject of a sentence, choose the correct verb form, recognise common agreement errors, and write more accurate sentences for IELTS Writing and Speaking.
