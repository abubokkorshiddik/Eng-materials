Subject–verb agreement

Master complex agreement patterns in academic English with tables and a ten-question quiz.

1. Relative clauses: “one of” versus “the only one”

PatternCorrect exampleExplanation
One of the + plural noun + whoShe is one of the researchers who work on this topic.Who refers to researchers; use the plural verb.
The only one of the + plural noun + whoShe is the only one of the researchers who works on this topic.The only one identifies one person; use the singular verb.

2. Inversion: the verb before the subject

In formal or literary structures, the subject may come after the verb. Find the subject before deciding on agreement.

SentenceSubjectVerb
Among the findings is a significant association.a significant associationis
Among the findings are several associations.several associationsare
On the table lies a book.a booklies
On the table lie three books.three bookslie

3. “A number of” versus “the number of”

PhraseAgreementExample
A number of = severalPluralA number of studies have reported this result.
The number of = the totalSingularThe number of studies has increased.

4. Percentages and fractions

The verb generally agrees with the noun after of.

SentenceReason
Sixty per cent of the population lives in urban areas.Population is treated as one collective total.
Sixty per cent of participants live in urban areas.Participants is plural.
One-third of the land is protected.Land is uncountable/singular.
One-third of the households are affected.Households is plural.

5. Titles and names

Titles of books, films, and works usually take a singular verb when they refer to one work, even if the title looks plural.

Great Expectations is a novel. The United Nations has issued a statement.

6. Gerund and infinitive phrases as subjects

A gerund or infinitive phrase used as the subject generally takes a singular verb when it represents one activity or idea.

Collecting reliable data is essential.

To reduce emissions requires coordinated action.

7. Academic writing: diagnose the real subject

IncorrectCorrectWhy
The results of the analysis indicates a trend.The results of the analysis indicate a trend.Results is plural.
The impact of the interventions vary.The impact of the interventions varies.Impact is singular.
The evidence suggest a change.The evidence suggests a change.Evidence is usually uncountable and singular.
A number of studies has shown this.A number of studies have shown this.A number of means several.

8. IELTS writing checklist

  1. Underline the main subject in long sentences.
  2. Ignore interrupting phrases when checking agreement.
  3. Check indefinite pronouns such as everyone and each.
  4. Check the noun after of in percentages and quantities.
  5. Proofread every verb in your final Task 1 or Task 2 response.
