In plain words

Every tense answers two questions. When? Past, present or future. What shape is the action? Choose the shape first, then the time, and the tense name follows.

Simple
The whole fact, habit or event.

Continuous
In progress, not finished.

Perfect
Finished before, or linked to, a reference time.

Perfect continuous
Duration up to a reference time.

How to read the timelines

The line goes from past (left) to future (right). The dashed line in the middle is now. A dot is one point in time. A bar is an action that lasts. A ring is a reference point, such as a past moment or a future deadline.

Quick way to choose

  • Is it a habit or a fact? Present simple.
  • Is it happening now, or around now? Present continuous.
  • Did it finish at a stated time? Past simple. Is there a link to now? Present perfect.
  • Two past events? The earlier one is past perfect. The later one is past simple.
  • Do you stress how long? Use a perfect continuous: present, past or future to match the reference point.
  • A deadline in the future? Future perfect, or future perfect continuous for duration.
