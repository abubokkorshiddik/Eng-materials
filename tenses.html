The tenses, in the order that makes sense

Start at the top and work down. Simple tenses first, then continuous, then perfect. Each lesson explains the idea in plain words, shows the mistakes people really make, and ends with a quiz. Present simple and present continuous share one lesson because you learn them best side by side.

  1. Present simple and present continuousPresent simple = facts, habits, schedules. Present continuous = happening now, temporary, changing.
  2. Past simpleUse the past simple for finished events, past states and past habits at a finished time.
  3. Past continuousUse it for an activity in progress at a past moment, or as background to a past event.
  4. Future simpleUse will for predictions, instant decisions, promises, offers and expectations.
  5. Future continuousAn activity will be in progress at a future moment, or an expected future event.
  6. Present perfectLink the past to now: experience, result, or a situation that continues. No finished time.
  7. Past perfectShow that one past event happened before another past event or time.
  8. Future perfectSomething will be completed before a future time or event.
  9. Present perfect continuousStress the activity and its duration: started in the past, continues or has just stopped.
  10. Past perfect continuousStress how long an activity had been going on before a past moment.
  11. Future perfect continuousStress how long an activity will have been going on by a future time.
