Grammar

Ten topics, from building blocks to finishing touches.

1. Sentence structure

Build complete, accurate sentences.

2. Subject–verb agreement

Avoid errors such as “people is” instead of “people are”.

3. Tenses

Choose the correct tense and keep time references consistent.
  1. One-page tense summary (timelines and quiz)
  2. Tenses overview
  3. Present simple and continuous
  4. Past simple
  5. Past continuous
  6. Future simple
  7. Future continuous
  8. Present perfect
  9. Past perfect
  10. Future perfect
  11. Present perfect continuous
  12. Past perfect continuous
  13. Future perfect continuous

4. Articles and nouns

Use a, an, the, and plural forms correctly.

5. Complex sentences

Connect ideas clearly and accurately.

6. Relative clauses

Add information without creating multiple short sentences.

7. Conditionals

Discuss possibilities, consequences, and hypothetical situations.

8. Passive voice

Useful for academic writing and process descriptions.

9. Gerunds and infinitives

Avoid errors such as “I enjoy to read” instead of “I enjoy reading”.

10. Punctuation

Separate ideas correctly and avoid comma splices.
