How to use this library

A simple way to build a consistent English learning routine.

1. Choose your level

Start with a resource that matches your current English level. Build a strong foundation before moving to advanced material.

2. Read and practise

Study a short section at a time. Write down unfamiliar words, review grammar examples and make your own sentences.

3. Review regularly

Return to your notes, repeat exercises and read English texts regularly to strengthen long-term learning.
