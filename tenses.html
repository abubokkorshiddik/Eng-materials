Your recommended learning order

Learn the basic forms first, then progress to continuous, perfect, and perfect continuous structures.

Stage 01 Simple tenses

Habits, facts, completed actions, and future events.

Stage 02 Continuous tenses

Actions in progress at a particular time.

Stage 03 Perfect tenses

Connections between actions and time.

Stage 04 Perfect continuous

Duration and ongoing activities across time.
